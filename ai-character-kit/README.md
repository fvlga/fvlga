# AI Character Starter Kit: Monsieur Gant

One original character, ten silent short scenes, and a fixed budget of $200–300. The goal of the test is to find out whether viewers want another episode.

## Files

| File | What it's for |
|---|---|
| `character-bible.md` | Who he is, his look, his rules, and what he never does |
| `prompts/character-sheet.md` | Image prompts for the portrait, full body, side view and three-quarter view |
| `prompts/video.md` | Motion-transfer and image-to-video prompt templates |
| `scenes.md` | The ten scenes: the shot, the motion to record, and the twist ending |
| `tracker.csv` | Posting log with views, completion rate, shares and profile visits |
| `scripts/generate_images.py` | Optional script that generates the character sheet through an image API |
| `refs/` | Put the chosen master image and your recorded motion clips here |
| `output/` | Generated images land here |

## Workflow for each scene

1. **Record the motion.** Film yourself for 5–8 seconds with a still camera, one person in frame and your hands clearly visible.
2. **Upload the references.** Use `refs/gant_master.png` for his appearance and your clip for the motion.
3. **Generate a few takes.** Plan on about 4 per scene.
4. **Pick the best take.** Check the face, the hands, the gloves and the bow tie.
5. **Edit.** Cut the dead time and open on the glove tug in the first second.

## Rules

- **Label every post as fictional and AI-generated.** Put it in the bio and use each platform's AI-content label.
- **Use only motion you recorded yourself.** Don't swap him into other people's viral videos, which risks copyright strikes and lost reach.
- **Keep him consistent.** Always reuse the master image as the reference, and never generate a new face.

## Tools (pick one of each)

- **Images:** ChatGPT Images or Nano Banana 2 (try Nano Banana Pro for hard angles)
- **Video:** Kling Motion Control or Higgsfield Genjutsu for motion transfer; Seedance 2.5 or Runway Gen-4 Turbo for image-to-video
- **Edit:** whatever editor you already know (CapCut works fine)

## Budget (10 clips)

| Item | Estimate |
|---|---|
| ~50 images | ~$4 |
| 320 s of video (10 clips × 4 takes × 8 s, at ~$0.32/s) | ~$102 |
| Other AI operations | $20 |
| Music and editing | up to $30 |
| **Planned total with a buffer** | **$200–300** |

Plan on 30–50 hours of your own time for the first round.

## Schedule

- **Days 1–3:** build the character sheet, then record and generate scenes 1–4.
- **Days 4–14:** post one scene every day or two, and log each post in `tracker.csv` 48 hours after it goes up.
- **After scene 10:** review the tracker. If completion rate and profile visits are rising, make ten more. If not, change the character or the format before spending more.
