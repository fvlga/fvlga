# The Plan: Monsieur Gant, First 3 Weeks

**Goal:** post 10 scenes in 14 days, then decide from the numbers whether to keep going.
**Budget cap:** $250 for tools. Stop and review before spending more.
**Time:** about 2–3 hours a day.

---

## Week 0: Setup (Days 1–4)

### Day 1: Accounts and tools (about 2 h)
- [ ] Pick a handle that's free on TikTok, Instagram **and** X (for example `@monsieur.gant`). Check all three before you sign up anywhere.
- [ ] Create all 3 accounts with the same name and the same bio: *"Concierge of the highest standards. Fictional AI character 🎩"*
- [ ] Sign up for **one** image tool (ChatGPT Images or Nano Banana 2).
- [ ] Sign up for **one** video tool. Kling Motion Control is a good first choice for motion transfer.
- [ ] Start a spending log. The `video_cost_usd` column in `tracker.csv` works for this.

### Day 2: Find his face (about 3 h)
- [ ] Run the "explore" prompt from `prompts/character-sheet.md` 5–10 times.
- [ ] Pick one image and save it as `refs/gant_master.png`. **This is his face from now on, so don't change it later.**
- [ ] Generate the 4 character-sheet views from the master image.
- [ ] Go through the consistency checklist and regenerate any view that fails.
- [ ] Crop the portrait to make the avatar, then upload it to all 3 accounts.

### Day 3: Record motion (about 2 h)
- [ ] Set up a phone on a stand, against a plain wall, with good light.
- [ ] Practise the glove tug (left, then right, slow and stiff) until it feels like *him*.
- [ ] Record motion clips for scenes **1, 2, 3, 6 and 9**. Do 3 takes of each, 5–8 seconds per take.
- [ ] Name the files `refs/motion/scene01_take1.mp4` and so on.

### Day 4: First generations (about 3 h)
- [ ] Generate scene 1. Do up to 4 takes, then stop.
- [ ] Check each take: face, gloves, bow tie, fingers.
- [ ] Edit the best take in your editor. Start on the glove tug and cut any dead time.
- [ ] **Checkpoint:** if scene 1 looks wrong after 4 takes, try the other video tool *before* you make scenes 2–10.
- [ ] Generate scenes 2 and 3.

3. Fill in `tracker.csv` for the post that went up **48 hours ago**.

## Week 3: Review and decide (Days 19–21)

### Day 20: Review (about 2 h)
Open `tracker.csv` and answer these questions:
- [ ] Which 2 scenes had the best **completion rate**? What do they have in common: the setting, the kind of twist, or the pigeon?
- [ ] Which scenes got **shares** and **profile visits**? These matter more than raw views.
- [ ] Are comments asking for more ("do one where he…")?
- [ ] Is the trend going **up** from scene 1 to scene 10?
- [ ] What did the test actually cost, in money and in hours?

### Day 21: Decide

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Profile visits and followers are rising, and people ask for more | **Keep going.** Make 10 more scenes in the style of your top 2. Budget another $200–300. |
| Some scenes hit and others flopped | **Narrow down.** Make 5 more scenes in the winning style only. |
| Flat everywhere, few shares | **Change one thing before spending more:** a new gesture, a new setting type, or a new character. Keep the workflow, since you've already learned it. |

---

## Ground rules (every day)
- Use the same face, outfit and glove tug every time. Never generate a new face.
- Use only motion you recorded yourself. Never take other people's videos.
- Label every post as AI-generated.
- Stay within the budget cap until Day 21.
- Make money only after you have an audience: ads, brand deals, merch. Skip the tokens.
