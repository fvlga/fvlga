# The Ten Scenes

Each scene follows the same pattern: **glove tug, then the task, then the twist, then a deadpan beat.**

- **Method:** **MT** means motion transfer (you record yourself). **I2V** means image to video.
- **Length:** each scene runs 6–8 seconds.

| # | Title | Setting | The task (what you record) | Twist ending | Method |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Bell** | Hotel front desk | Tug the gloves, then press the desk bell once, precisely | A tiny string quartet rises from behind the desk and plays one chord. He nods once. | MT |
| 2 | **The Jar** | Kitchen counter | Tug the gloves, then try to open a jar, straining but dignified | He sets it down and whistles. A pigeon lands and opens it. He bows to the pigeon. | MT |
| 3 | **The Key** | Hotel corridor | Tug the gloves, then reach into your coat and present a key with both hands | The key is 3 feet long. He hands it over without a flicker. | MT |
| 4 | **The Door** | Revolving door | Tug the gloves, then push the revolving door for a guest | He keeps it going, faster and faster, perfectly composed, and the guest never comes out. | I2V |
| 5 | **The Umbrella** | Street in the rain | Tug the gloves, then open an umbrella over someone off-screen | The umbrella opens over a tiny dog. The rain stops everywhere except on him. | MT |
| 6 | **The Tea** | Lounge table | Tug the gloves, then pour tea from high up, then higher | The stream reaches all the way from the ceiling and the cup fills perfectly. Eyebrow raise. | MT |
| 7 | **The Lift** | Elevator doors | Tug the gloves, then hold the elevator door and gesture "after you" | The door opens on a dumbwaiter. He climbs in anyway, folding himself neatly. | MT |
| 8 | **Lost & Found** | Desk with a box | Tug the gloves, then dig through a lost-and-found box | He pulls out a full-size bicycle, then a canoe paddle, then a smaller version of himself. | I2V |
| 9 | **The Crumb** | Restaurant table | Tug the gloves, then spot one crumb and remove it with tweezers | He places it in a velvet box, snaps it shut and hands it to the guest. | MT |
| 10 | **Day Off** | Park bench | Tug the gloves, then sit and try to relax, unable to slouch | A pigeon lands. He immediately tugs his gloves and begins brushing its feathers. | MT |

## Order to post

Post the strongest hooks first: **1 → 2 → 3 → 6 → 9 → 5 → 7 → 4 → 8 → 10.**

## Caption pattern

Keep captions short, and don't put any text on the video itself. Example: *"Standards must be maintained. 🎩 #aicharacter"*

## Variations if a scene works

Keep the character exactly the same and change one thing: the location, what he does after the glove tug, or the ending. Scene 2 and Scene 10 both feature the pigeon, which could become a recurring sidekick.
