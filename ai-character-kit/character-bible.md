# Character Bible: Monsieur Gant

## Concept

Monsieur Gant is an impossibly serious hotel concierge who treats every trivial task as a matter of the highest ceremony. The comedy comes from the gap between how much he cares and how little the situation matters. He never speaks.

## Identity (lock these and never change them)

| Trait | Detail |
|---|---|
| Age | Looks about 60 |
| Build | Tall and thin, with an upright, almost rigid posture |
| Face | Long and narrow, with a pencil-thin grey moustache, heavy eyebrows and a permanently unimpressed expression |
| Hair | Silver, slicked straight back |
| **Signature detail** | **A huge burgundy velvet bow tie, about twice normal size** |
| Outfit | Black tailcoat with brass buttons, grey waistcoat, white shirt, pressed black trousers and polished black shoes |
| **Hands** | **Spotless white cotton gloves, always on** |

## Recurring gesture (the hook)

Before any task, he **tugs each glove tight, finger by finger: left hand first, then right.** It always opens the clip and lasts about 1 second.

## Personality

- Formal, precise and proud.
- He is never surprised. When something absurd happens, he raises one eyebrow at most.
- He always finishes the job, however strange it gets.
- He has a soft spot for pigeons, which could become a running gag.

## What he never does

- Speak, laugh, or show his bare hands.
- Wear anything other than the outfit above.
- Appear without the bow tie.
- Look at the camera, except for one slow glance at the end of a scene, used sparingly.

## Scene formula

**Glove tug** (0–1 s) → **an ordinary task** (1–5 s) → **an unexpected ending** (5–8 s) → **a deadpan beat**, usually an eyebrow raise or a bow.
