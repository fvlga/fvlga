# FVLGA · CUFF NO DANCER: Gemini prompts, round 3

New look for the ad-libs, "Japan" and the title. The words now live **inside dreamy 1960s outdoor film scenes**, matching the found footage of the boy and people outside, instead of big type on a pattern.

How to use it: start a new Gemini chat, paste the **STYLE BASE** once, then one prompt at a time. Make 2–3 versions of each.
If the spelling comes out wrong, reply "fix the spelling to exactly: ___". If it adds a border, reply "full-bleed, no border, not a card".

## STYLE BASE (paste once)
> I'm making still frames for an underground rap music video. Each image is a **single full-bleed 16:9 film frame, 1920x1080 landscape**: not a card, poster, mockup or collage, with no borders. The look is a **dreamy faded 1960s 16mm home movie shot outdoors**: warm washed-out colour, golden-hour light, pink and orange light leaks, soft focus, heavy film grain, a slight gate weave, and dust specks. One word appears **physically inside the scene** as if it really exists there, never as a flat graphic laid on top. Everything is original: no logos, no recognisable real people, no visible faces (figures are far away, from behind, or in silhouette).

## Ad-libs (one image each)
**huh**
> Full-bleed 1960s 16mm film frame: a huge pale summer sky over a field, and a small vintage biplane far away has just finished **skywriting the word "huh"** in soft white smoke letters that are starting to drift and blur. A lone kid in a red t-shirt stands in the tall grass looking up, seen from behind. Warm faded colour, light leak in the corner, grain.

**yah**
> Full-bleed 1960s 16mm film frame: a sun-bleached brick wall beside a dirt road at golden hour, with the word **"yah"** spray-painted large in drippy hot-pink paint. A boy on a bicycle rides past in motion blur. Long shadows, faded colour, dust specks, grain.

**uh**
> Full-bleed 1960s 16mm film frame at dusk: an old roadside motel sign glowing in the purple sky, its flickering neon tubes spelling **"uh"** in pink cursive, with a few lights burnt out. Telephone wires, first stars, warm orange window light below. Soft focus, halation glow around the neon, grain.

## Japan (make both)
> **Version A:** a full-bleed 1960s 16mm film frame of a vintage travel poster pasted on a weathered wooden fence in a summer field, half peeled and flapping in the wind. It shows a red rising sun over a mountain, with the word **"Japan"** in elegant italic serif. Golden-hour light, grass in the foreground, grain.
> **Version B:** a full-bleed 1960s 16mm film frame from a moving train window at sunset: rice fields and a distant red torii gate. The word **"Japan"** is reflected faintly in the glass, as if written on the window in condensation, in italic script. Pink light leak, motion blur, grain.

## Title (used once, in the intro)
> Full-bleed 1960s 16mm film frame: a golden-hour field with tall grass blowing, and the words **"cuff no dancer"** standing in the field as **giant liquid-chrome letters**, lowercase elegant italic script, reflecting the sunset. A small figure walks away between the letters, seen from behind. Dreamy, faded, pink light leaks, grain.
