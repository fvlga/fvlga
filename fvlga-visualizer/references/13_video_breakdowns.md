## 1. Nettspend — "shut up" (Official Video)
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oiZ7VGjnqo4 · 2:06 · 2.2M views · Nettspend official channel
![frame](sandbox://workspace/user/media_library/browser_screenshots/8b/8b4f3c367af03e3cfb978df98370e115434d1bf6c838879e44c63625cefc02c1.png)
- **Grade:** Pure black-and-white, extreme contrast, crushed blacks, blown highlights. Underwater shots have a soft diffused glow; fine grain throughout.
- **Text:** None burned in.
- **Transitions:** Slow liquid dissolves — images melt into each other like ink in water. Hard cuts only on beat switches.
- **VFX:** Ink-in-water macro photography composited with performance; negative/invert face flips dissolving into ink; underwater distortion and bubbles; light-leak glow.
- **Location/performance:** Underwater tank — long wet hair over the face, floating, hands pressed to glass. Deadpan, drifting, eyes rarely open. Anti-performance, lethargic.
- **Read:** One conceit executed fully: ink + water + body. B&W makes skin, ink, and water a single texture; the underwater lethargy mirrors the mumbled vocal. The face-invert dissolve is the signature beat. The only fully B&W video and the only texture study with no location cuts in the set.

## Cross-video patterns (the formula)
1. **Hard cuts rule.** Every video cuts on the beat; dissolves appear only in the two slowest/softest videos (Nettspend's). Confirms the Bible's 70-20-10 rule.
2. **One aggressive grade per video.** B&W (shut up), night vision (trauma), full green (Sky), teal wash (lonely), red/teal split (Cha Ching). The grade IS the VFX in this scene — most videos have almost no composited effects beyond the grade.
3. **Text does the branding.** Karaoke captions (lonely), graffiti titles (che), persistent watermarks (Lyrical Lemonade milk carton, Lil Flxr badge, Autumn's red logo).
4. **Two poles:** meme-collage maximalism (Cha Ching) vs one-conceit minimalism (trauma's night vision, shut up's ink/water, Sky's green bodega). The best pick ONE trick and commit.
5. **Performance > choreography** — except 2hollis, everyone mugs/rages to camera rather than dancing.
6. **Lo-fi damage as texture:** film scratches, VHS lines, compression artifacts (Che, Cha Ching, Autumn) — degradation is a deliberate finish, not a flaw.
