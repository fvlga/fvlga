## Shared identity (as reported)
1. One recurring cartoon persona anchors the set; the red spiky/dread hair is the identifier; each track RE-COSTUMES him (brawler, knight, pixel sprite, varsity dancer) instead of redesigning.
2. Red is the brand colour (hair, glow outlines, the whole #5 void).
3. Reference-first art direction: the lyrics pick the cartoon/game/show.
4. Two render tiers (lo-fi trippy low-poly and smooth cartoon 3D) unified by persona and palette, not technique.
5. Karaoke typography is the house device, except the flagship single which drops text entirely.
6. Motion is drift, not cuts; hard cuts only as punctuation (e.g. the black lyric-drop frames in #3).
7. Every video opens cold on the world and closes on a hero close-up or icon (knight face, pendant, single heart).

## What we can steal, translated to OUR rules
- A recurring persona/mascot re-costumed per track (we have no cartoon persona yet; any must be drawn in code/vector/pixel or from public-domain art; NO AI images).
- A single brand colour stage: one flat saturated colour void for a whole video or a whole hit moment (example for us: one slime-green or one tomato-red stage with only the artist's graphics in it).
- Lyric-as-reference art direction when a song name-drops a world (for CUT HER MIC the name-drops are Summer Walker, Thugger, GLE, Chrome Hearts: a possible 'lookbook/lot' world).
- Black-frame beat-drop punctuation: a hard cut to black with ONE oversized word on a drop.
- Drift between punctuation cuts (slow continuous pushes instead of a cut every beat).
- A text-free hero moment for the biggest hook.
- Open cold on the world, close on one hero icon (our version: the sunset + care label / the pendant-equivalent).
- NOT borrowable: black outlines on lyric text (artist rule: no outlines on words), AI images, more than 2 font families, other artists' characters.
