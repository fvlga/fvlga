# Techniques A: how 10 underground-rap videos were made

Research limits (read first):
- Web search worked; YouTube and most article sites could not be fetched by the page tool, so text pages (Wikipedia, IMVDb, shots.net, UMG press, Uproxx, FADER) were read via plain HTTP.
- No video or media was downloaded. Nothing was watched frame by frame.
- Almost no source describes edit technique, transitions or tools for these videos. Credits are sparse. Tags used:
  - **VERIFIED** = a source says it explicitly (URL given).
  - **INFERRED** = general knowledge of the genre or the artist's visual language, not confirmed for this specific video. Treat as hypotheses to check by eye against the official video.
- Today's date context: Oct 2026. Several credits below are from 2026 pages.

## Clearest visual patterns across the 10 (from frames)
1. Blown-out light as a main effect: flash, bloom, lens-flare streaks and white-hot practical lights (2hollis, Ken Carson, kankan, Xavier sky, Nettspend upload face lighting).
2. Crushed blacks and a dominant single-hue grade: cold teal/slate (osamason sea, Destroy Lonely), warm amber on black (kankan, Ken Carson), mustard yellow (osamason).
3. Cheap-camera texture on purpose: soft, compressed, wide-lens close handheld (Xavier, 2hollis, kankan).
4. Motion blur and ghost frames instead of clean glitch: slow-shutter smear, double exposure and radial zoom blur (osamason, Xavier, Ken Carson).
5. Typography as a graphic object: torn-paper text, lime title cards, subtitle lines, chrome 3D logotype (osamason, kankan, che-upload).
