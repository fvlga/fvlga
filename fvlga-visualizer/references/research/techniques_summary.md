## Most common techniques (counts mix VERIFIED and INFERRED — weak signal)
1. Hard cuts locked to the beat, few designed transitions — about 8 of 10 in each group (mostly inferred). Matches the 70-20-10 rule.
2. Lo-fi / grain / night-vision texture — about 6 of 10 (Ken Carson, Carti, Lucki verified).
3. Glitch / digital distortion — about 6 of 10 (Ken Carson verified).
4. One strong colour world per section or release — about 5 of 10 (Homixide Gang, Carti verified).
5. Handheld phone / camcorder footage — about 3–8 depending on group (Chief Keef verified).
6. Superimposed cutouts / collage layering — Lucki verified.
7. Minimal or no on-screen text in most (inferred) — our video uses much more type than most of these.
8. Cole Bennett / Lyrical Lemonade link on several (Ken Carson, nettspend, SoFaygo (weak source), Che) — the "seamless object wipe" in the VFX doc.
