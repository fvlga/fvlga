## How to use
1. **Start a new Gemini chat and paste the STYLE BASE first.** Then paste one card prompt at a time.
2. **Make 2–3 versions of each card.** Pick your favorite, or send me all of them, since more variety means fewer repeats in the video.
3. **Ask for 16:9 landscape.** Use "1920x1080 landscape" in the prompt. I put the strip inside the vertical 9:16 frame.
4. **Spelling.** If a word comes out misspelled, reply "fix the spelling to exactly: CUFF NO DANCER" (or whichever word it is).
5. **Layers.** For prompts that say *on pure black background*, keep it pure black. I use the black to layer the art over footage and patterns.
6. **Download the images and send them here.** Tell me which card number each one is, or just name the files like `card1_v2.png`.
