## STYLE BASE (paste once)
> I'm making still frames for an underground rap music video for the artist **FVLGA**, song **"cuff no dancer"**. Each one is a **single full-bleed 16:9 music video frame, 1920x1080 landscape**. It is NOT a card, poster, tarot card or mockup: no borders, no frame edges, no margins. The look is dreamy found footage meets fashion editorial. Lettering is **sexy and elegant: high-contrast italic serif (like Didot or Bodoni italic) and flowing calligraphic script**, sometimes liquid chrome. Never blocky, condensed or bold sans-serif. Colours are acid green, hot pink, deep purple and black, with heavy film grain, soft glow, light leaks and slight chromatic aberration. Everything is original: no logos, no real people's faces. It should look expensive and handmade, not like clip-art or a template.

## 10 · Pink starburst

On the right, **"cuff no dancer"** in elegant white italic Didot-style serif, lowercase, with a soft pink glow. Tiny white four-point sparkles scattered around. Dreamy, glossy, sexy, grainy.

## 15 · Drop-out (alone)
