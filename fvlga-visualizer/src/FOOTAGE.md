# Footage
Clips are cut from two public-domain Prelinger Archives films (archive.org, licenseurl creativecommons.org/licenses/publicdomain):
- Journey through a Day (1967) — archive.org/details/Journeyt1967
- No Time for Ugliness, Part I — archive.org/details/no_time_for_ugliness_1
